Privacy Policy — Peakcut-clips

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Peakcut-clips is a personal tool that turns livestreams and videos into short vertical clips and, with the account owner's permission, posts those clips to the owner's own TikTok account using TikTok's official Content Posting API.

What we collect

  • TikTok account identifiers (open ID, username, display name) provided by TikTok when you connect your account.
  • An access token and refresh token issued by TikTok, used only to post videos you have chosen to your own account.
  • The video files and captions you choose to post through the tool.

What we do not collect

  • We never ask for or store your TikTok password.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

How data is used

Your data is used only to operate the posting feature: signing you in with TikTok Login Kit and publishing your selected videos to your own TikTok profile via the Content Posting API (scopes user.info.basic and video.publish).

Data storage and deletion

Tokens are stored privately on the operator's own server and are used only for the purposes above. You can disconnect the app at any time from within the tool or from your TikTok account settings, which stops all future posting. To request deletion of stored data, contact the operator at the email below.
